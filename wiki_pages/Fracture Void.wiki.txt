== Overview ==
'''Fracture Void''' is a {{Legendary}} level 380 katana, purchasable from the [[Helmfirth Expert Shop]] in [[Helmfirth]]. It has a 35% critical chance and does 29,000 damage. When [[Upgrading|upgraded]] to +20, it does 58,000 damage. In addition, it grants the user a +45% health regeneration buff and +50% stamina regeneration buff.
