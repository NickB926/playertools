== Overview ==
'''Void Piercer''' is a {{Legendary}} level 250 spear, purchasable from the [[Helmfirth Expert Shop]] in [[Helmfirth]]. It has a 24% critical chance and does 7,600 damage. When [[Upgrading|upgraded]] to +20, it does 15,200 damage. In addition, it grants the user a +16% health regeneration buff and +18% stamina regeneration buff.
